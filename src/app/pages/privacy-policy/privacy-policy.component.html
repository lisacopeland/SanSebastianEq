Privacy Policy

At San Sebastian Equestrian Center, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

We collect information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any details you include in our contact form. We may also collect limited technical information through third-party services such as Google Maps.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide location and directions via Google Maps

Third-Party Services

Our website may use Google Maps to display our location. Google may collect data such as IP address or browser details when loading the map. Please refer to Google’s Privacy Policy for more details.

Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising. Some cookies may be placed by third-party services (such as Google Maps) when used on this site.
